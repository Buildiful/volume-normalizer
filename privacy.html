YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy at Buildiful®

Volume Normalizer works without accounts, subscriptions, or uploading audio files.

Last updated: November 2026

Local processing

When you select a source folder, audio is read and processed locally by your browser. Generated files are saved to a folder you choose or packaged into a ZIP in your browser. We do not receive, store, or host your audio, filenames, folder paths, metadata, or export files.

Optional analytics

With your permission, this tool loads Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to learn which tool features are used, such as starting analysis and completing exports. We only send aggregate numeric usage parameters, broad output format, and whether folder or ZIP export was used. We do not include filenames, paths, audio, audio metadata, or precise file sizes in these events.

Google Analytics may use cookies and process page visits, browser and device information, and approximate location. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled for this integration.

Declining optional analytics prevents the Google Analytics script from loading on subsequent page views. The tool works either way. Consent and a timestamp are saved in browser local storage. You can reopen the choice via Analytics settings in the app footer. Declining after opting in prevents future consented collection, but does not delete data already collected by Google.

Optional MP3 encoder

When you choose MP3 output, your browser loads the open-source lamejs encoder from jsDelivr. The CDN receives a normal request for a JavaScript library, but audio is encoded on your device and is not uploaded to the CDN. WAV output needs no external encoder download.

Files and browser permissions

Folder access is requested only when you click the relevant browse button. For directory exports, your browser grants this page access to the destination you select. You can revoke file permissions from your browser's site settings. We recommend Chrome or Edge for directory-to-directory workflows.

Third-party hosting

The project can be hosted via GitHub Pages. GitHub's standard hosting logs and policies may apply when you visit the website. External links have their own privacy practices.
