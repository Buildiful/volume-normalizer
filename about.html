Volume Normalizer

Keep volume consistent across your music and audio recordings.

Volume Normalizer is a free, open-source batch loudness normalizer. Choose audio files from a folder, analyze perceived loudness, and export normalized copies. The original files remain untouched.

How it works

The tool measures each recording using ITU-R BS.1770 K-weighted loudness metering with EBU R128-style gating. It calculates one gain adjustment per track to preserve musical dynamics and uses a sample-peak ceiling to avoid digital sample clipping. A track with strong peaks may end up below the requested LUFS target.

Volume Normalizer does not use dynamic compression or limiting, and it is not a certified true-peak limiter.

Files stay on your device

Audio is decoded, measured, and exported locally in your browser. No audio is uploaded. Existing files are not overwritten; duplicate filenames simply get a numeric suffix. Optional Google Analytics runs only with consent and does not receive your filenames or audio. Read the privacy notice for details.

Browser and formats

Chrome or Edge is recommended for folder selection and direct destination-folder exports. Other supported browsers can import files and export ZIP downloads, depending on their capabilities and memory limits.

Inputs depend on browser decoding support. WAV exports require no additional encoder download. MP3 exports load an optional external encoder and may introduce some generational quality loss when re-encoding compressed audio.
